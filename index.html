Who we are

A company in its own right.

Human Rights Angels Registry Inc. is a C-corporation with its own governance, records, and commercial operations. It handles the for-profit ventures that Micdom AI Records does not, alongside its own corporate registry.

Think of the character as registry-grade administration paired with serious systems engineering, carrying an ecological preservation mission. That is a description of our design philosophy only; it does not imply affiliation with any other organization.

“Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish the earth, and subdue it: and have dominion.” — Genesis 1:28 (KJV). This is the founder’s guiding vision for eco-stewardship; it does not make the company a charity.

Operating principles

Retained proxy, separate entity

Interface architecture

Metatron: the Interface’d Maestro

Metatron is the public-facing interface and orchestration layer. HALLEL and other specialized agents are the supporting cast behind it.

METATRON · Interface

Welcomes visitors, explains pathways, and routes people to the right human contact, form, record, or service.

HALLEL · Supporting agents
